Our mission

Building a Circular Future for Electronics

RENVO was founded on a simple idea: the gadgets we outgrow still have real value. We connect people who want to sell with people who want to save, creating a marketplace that is good for wallets and good for the planet.

By making it easy to buy and sell pre-owned devices, we extend the life of electronics, reduce e-waste, and give more people access to the technology they love.
